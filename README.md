Neon Bay is an original top-down open-world crime game about a coastal city where bright beaches hide a busy underground. Walk the neighborhoods, borrow a car without asking, and make your own trouble while the police close in.

## Story

Local fixer Sol Vega has a plan to expose the people quietly buying up the waterfront. Take jobs across the city, recover evidence, and help Sol send the story beyond the bay. Each job pays cash and opens the next chapter.

## Controls

- WASD or arrow keys: move on foot; accelerate, brake, and steer in a car.
- E: enter or leave a nearby car; interact with mission markers.
- Mouse: aim; click to fire. Space: punch.
- L: mission log. M: mute or unmute. Escape: pause.

## Features

Explore distinct districts, beaches, parks, and the marina with moving traffic and pedestrians. Steal cars, evade responding police, fight back, and follow a six-part story. The HUD includes a minimap, health, cash, wanted stars, objective, and driving speed. Progress is saved locally in your browser. Sound is synthesized in the browser; the game needs no downloads or network once its files are loaded.

## Run it

Run `node serve.ts` from the repository root and open the local address printed by the server. Alternatively, serve the `game/` directory with any static web server. Desktop Chrome and a keyboard and mouse are recommended.
